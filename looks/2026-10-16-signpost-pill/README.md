## The design

One highlight treatment everywhere: B's burnt-orange pill marks whatever he is saying or naming right now.

- **Revised 16 Oct after Josh's review of cut v3** ("your subtitles and pills and highlights on terminal stuff do NOT properly cover words and look WRONG"): the pill no longer slides. It snaps to each word the frame that word starts (from the forced alignment), covers that word's whole glyph box (ascenders and descenders) with even padding on all sides, and covers nothing else; it stays on the word until the next word starts. The same for terminal highlights (exactly the named text's character cells, plus even padding) and the typed words in the Claude Code box.
- **Phrasing (revised 17 Oct):** the edit gives every subtitle phrase (each line's `subtitles` in `edit.json`), one or two lines, at most about 20 characters a line; the look renders them as given. Josh, 17 Oct: "i mean you can also have multiple lines on screen?" Two-line phrases sit centred on the layout's subtitle height, line height 1.1, and the pill walks through the words across both lines.
- **Subtitles:** A's type: Futura Bold, 80 px, white, sentence case, soft shadow `0 4px 18px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`, no outline (the Resolve template has the shadow only; an outline tried on 22 Oct rendered as a harder shadow and did not help captions over a light app, `2026-10-12-codex-switch-run1/versions/look-v1-captions-B-real.png`: frame a light picture so the named line sits above the band instead), no box. Phrases as the edit gives them, centred at the layout's subtitle height. The word being spoken sits on B's pill (`#D9531E`, corner radius half its height, white text), snapping from word to word on his word timings, so the line walks the viewer through what he says. Phrases cut in and out.
- **Terminal:** A's warm near-black `#15120E` at 94%, text `#EDE5D3`, dim `#7E776A`, JetBrains Mono fitted to the column count the edit gives (36 on the first video), rounded corners 18 px, inset 36 px. Highlights use the same orange pill behind the named text (white text on it), appearing on the frame he names it. No zoom: the column count is chosen so the text reads on a phone, wrapping as the tool wraps it.
- **Claude Code moment:** the same terminal colours, the input box text as large as fits the whole request, and each typed word lit with the same orange pill as he says it, snapping word to word like the subtitles.
- **Photo labels:** much bigger and readable at a glance: Futura Bold 52 px, white, "📍 " before the place name, on a dark rounded backing `rgba(0,0,0,0.55)` with 12 px padding, at the top-left of each tile, wrapping to at most two lines.
- **Closing page (from cut v19, replacing the end card):** one page over his footage as it runs on after his last word, not a held frame. The footage darkens to 20% behind it over 10 frames, then becomes the plain background over 12; the words fade in over 3. Centred between y 160 and y 1620 with 60 px side margins: the headline in Futura Bold 100 px; the ask in Futura Medium 58 px, `#EBEBEB`; the prompt in an opaque box in the terminal's colours, Film Mono 46 px with a 2 px border; the call to action in Futura Bold 54 px on the orange pill; the mission in Futura Bold 50 px, `#E1E1E1`. The values are `card` in the module.
- **Colour feel for the grade:** A's: warm and gentle, a touch golden, soft contrast.

## Native Resolve subtitles (added 17 Oct by Claude Opus 5.5, for cut v9)

`resolve/Signpost Pill Subtitles.setting` is this look as a Resolve subtitle template, made from Resolve 21.1's stock "Word Highlight" (manual ch. 59, "AI Animated Subtitles"). Futura Bold, white, the size of 80 px type in a 1080 px wide frame (Size 0.117); the word being spoken on the orange pill with fully rounded ends (Round 1.0) and white text (Highlight Fill on, white: with it off the spoken word's text disappears); centred at y 1470 of 1080x1920 (Center Y 0.234375, Fusion measures from the bottom).

- **One position per track.** A template on a subtitle track overrides track and per-caption styling (manual ch. 59, p. 1242), so every layout puts its subtitle band at the same height: y 1470.
- **Colour.** Fusion titles work in the timeline's scene-linear colour space, so a display colour typed in comes out wrong (#D9531E came out as (252,136,0)). The template's pill is #D9531E converted from Rec.709 to DaVinci Wide Gamut linear, the same conversion the graphics' colour node makes: (0.44925, 0.15145, 0.09010); on the pipeline's timelines (inverse DRT on) it rendered as exactly (217, 83, 30) on 22 Oct (`.cache/captions-native/colour-test.json`). The 17 Oct values (0.7492, 0.1648, 0.0923), solved from a chart on a timeline without the inverse DRT, rendered as (255, 69, 0) there: two oranges in one video, the fault the first codex-switch run found. The same values are in `caption-track.drt` (its embedded copy of the template) and in the `.setting`; change both together, and measure one rendered pill before a cut goes out.
- **Install.** Copy the file into `~/Library/Application Support/Blackmagic Design/DaVinci Resolve/Fusion/Templates/Edit/Titles/` and restart Resolve. It appears under Effects > Titles > Fusion Titles. Apply it with a slow drag (about 2.5 s) onto the subtitle track header.
- **Pill size** (22 Oct, Claude Fable 5.1). The pill's Height is -0.012 with an Offset Y of -0.003 (template inputs HBackHeight, HBackOffsetY): rendered, the pill covers the descenders of g and y with about 4 px to spare and keeps about 30 px above the ascenders (`.cache/captions-native/pill-A-crop.png`). At the old -0.018 the tail of a y poked out of the pill's curved end, against the rule that the highlight covers the whole glyph box.
- **Caption track file** (added 22 Oct by Claude Fable 5.1). `resolve/caption-track.drt` is a timeline exported from Resolve with this template on its subtitle track and nothing else. `pipeline/resolve/add_captions.py` copies that track, with a cut's captions written into it, into the cut's own timeline file, so the drag is done once per look and never per cut. When the template changes: drop the new one on a subtitle track once, export that timeline (`Timeline.Export` with `EXPORT_DRT`), and save it here under this name; `pipeline/subtitles/write_caption_track.py` reads only the subtitle track from it.

## Changes of 26 Oct (added by Claude Opus 5.5, look and layout, hill-climb stream C)

From the faults of the codex-switch short's run 3 (`video-skills-redesign/2026-10-21-fable/poc/faults-run3.md`, entries 14 to 16, 23 and 24) and its cold judgement (`poc/side-by-side/judgement.md`). The folder as it was is in `resolve/backup-2026-10-26/`, with the Fusion templates and `template_kinds.py` from `pipeline/b/` under `pipeline-b-templates/`. Proof frames: `poc/hillclimb/C-work/check1/`.

- **Caption shadow behind the pill.** The caption template drew the spoken word's text shadow on top of its pill, so the pill read darker than the look's orange (about (209, 81, 30), and (193, 76, 30) next to the letters). The shadow element now sits behind the pill (`PriorityBack8 = -10` in `resolve/Signpost Pill Subtitles.setting` and in the copy inside `resolve/caption-track.drt`; the installed copy in Resolve's Templates folder is the same file). Rendered, every caption pill is flat (217, 83, 30).
- **Title pill.** Its side padding is 0.27 em (was 0.17): at 0.17 the first and last letters of a lit word poked up to 1.5 px out of the pill's round ends. No more spaces inside the `[[ ]]` to widen it.
- **Closing page address size.** `Signpost Closing` has an `AddressSize` input (a Text+ Size: px / 100 x 0.15063). A full `github.com/owner/repository` fits one line at 40 px (0.060252), its pill clear of the button column.
- **Opening title.** `Signpost Opening` is the title as the video's first card: 110 px, its lines centred on y 380, the title's dark band on. A title at the caption height reads as a caption arriving ahead of his voice (three reviewers read it that way in run 3); high, large and on its band it reads as a card. Values: `fusion.title.opening`.
- **Highlight on app pixels.** `Signpost Highlight` draws an outline in the pill's orange round a region of a real screen recording, from the frame he names it, until the next highlight or the shot's end. Inputs: `RegionLeft`, `RegionTop`, `RegionRight`, `RegionBottom` (the region's ink box, inclusive frame pixels from the top left) and `Padding`. Values in `fusion.highlight`: the outline's inner edge 10 px outside the ink, 6 px stroke (about 2 px on a phone), inner corner radius 24 px capped at half the inner height, so a one-line region gets the captions' pill shape. Where the app's own edge is closer than padding plus stroke, lower `Padding` to no less than 6. Why an outline: the run tested an outline against dimming the panel outside the region; dimming made the target stand out most but greyed the real data round it, while the outline keeps the app's pixels untouched and ties the lit caption word to the same orange round the thing on screen. A filled pill would hide what it marks. Three reviewers in that run liked it; Josh has not yet seen it.
- **Captions against his face.** `subtitles.faceClearance`: the caption band's top must be at least 120 px below his lower lip, so in every layout his lower lip's lowest point is at or above y 1252 (two-line captions; 1304 for one line). With a panel above him ending at y P, his eyes P + headroom below it and his eye-to-lower-lip distance d at that framing, the panel must end at or above y 1252 - headroom - d: about y 607 to 707 at run 3's framings. On run 3's cut this flags exactly the judge's six "captions over his face" stretches and nothing he passed; on run 2's cut, exactly its one.

## Change of 27 Oct (added by Claude Opus 5.5, motion on codex-switch run 4)

- **Closing page fade timings are inputs.** `Signpost Closing` publishes `DarkFrames`, `DarkTo` and `PlainFrames` (defaults the look's `fusion.closing.fade`: 10, 0.8, 12, so an unchanged row renders as before; checked on the darkening curve frame by frame). The background's alpha is an expression of them: linear to `DarkTo` over `DarkFrames`, then to 1 over `PlainFrames`.
- **Why.** At the phone budget (28 MB, about 2.0 Mbit/s for 95 s) the default's 13 frames of a near-black face (80% to 100% dark) broke into large blocks in Resolve's H.264 (codex-switch, frames 2243 to 2256, found by a cold judge). With 9, 0.9, 1 the footage reaches the plain background 10 frames in and the blocks are gone; the page and its fade-in are otherwise the same. Proof: `2026-10-12-codex-switch/scratch/motion/check/fade/` (compare.png, compare2.png, blocks.txt).
- **Test it on a full-length phone render.** A `--range` phone render sets its bitrate from the range's length (`render_timeline.py`), so a short span encodes at about 30 Mbit/s and hides this.
- Made by `template_kinds.py` and `make_templates.py`; the previous file and `template_kinds.py` are in `pipeline/b/templates/2026-10-16-signpost-pill/backup-2026-10-27-motion/`.
