# Look options for the where-was-i short

Three complete looks. Each covers everything drawn on screen: subtitles, the terminal and its highlight, photo labels, the end card and how things move. Each is one module of values; the components, content, cues and layout do not change between them. The picture is the same ungraded, colour-managed picture in all three, because the grade comes after the pick; each look's colour feel is described in words for the colourist.

What Josh has said that they answer: subtitles "much BIGGER", "twitter style", readable with the sound off; titles "much bigger and less dim"; subtitles not "confused with title font"; one "HOLISTIC style that matches BOTH the subtitles AND the various title/subtitle/on screen graphics and is COHERENT"; "stylistically coherent but tasteful"; the terminal "look real but fit the vertical format". His moodboard marks one reel for subtitles and one for titles (the Hong Kong night-food reel and the Mandarin morning reel, `looks/moodboard/videos/DcPmhAAMjTG.mp4` and `DctJbceTsrg.mp4`).

Sizes are pixels on the 1080 by 1920 frame. "Safe" means between y 200 and y 1560, and clear of the right-hand 130 px from y 900 down.

## A. Signpost (from the Hong Kong reel)

The reel's quiet geometric type and warm yellow, scaled up for a phone.

- **Subtitles:** Futura Bold, 80 px, line height 1.1, white, sentence case, no box. Soft shadow `0 4px 18px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)` plus a 2 px outline `rgba(0,0,0,0.35)` so they hold on the bright cupboard. One or two lines, at most 22 characters a line, centred, the block's centre at y 1480. Phrases cut in and out with no animation.
- **Labels and titles** (photo place names, the end card): Futura Medium, two-tier like the reel's "Sing Kee / 63 Stanley St": the name at 52 px, a second line at 30 px where the content has one (for photos, the date taken). Top left at x 60, y 230 on photos. White, same shadow as subtitles.
- **Accent:** warm yellow `#F6CF4A`, used only for highlights.
- **Terminal:** warm near-black `#15120E` at 94%, text `#EDE5D3`, dim text and prompt `#7E776A`, JetBrains Mono at the size that fits 44 columns in the panel with 44 px side padding, line height 1.35. Rounded corners 18 px, the panel inset 36 px from the frame edges, no title bar. Highlight: the target's text turns yellow and a 5 px yellow underline draws under it left to right over 6 frames; the previous highlight eases back to the text colour over 6 frames.
- **End card:** over his held last frame, dimmed 45%. Josh's words in Futura Medium 72 px, the link `github.com/opentrawl/where-was-i` below in Futura Medium 50 px, left-aligned at x 60, vertically centred.
- **Motion:** quiet. Terminal scroll 12 frames, ease-out cubic. Labels fade in over 6 frames.
- **Colour feel:** warm and gentle, a touch golden, soft contrast, like the reel's street at night.

## B. Pill (from the Mandarin reel)

The reel's bold coloured pill, made the one treatment for everything that is emphasised: the subtitle and the terminal highlight are the same shape and colour.

- **Subtitles:** SF Pro Rounded Heavy (weight 800), 76 px, white, on a burnt-orange pill `#D9531E`, corner radius half the pill's height, padding 14 px top and bottom, 30 px sides. Each line is its own pill, at most 18 characters a line, one or two lines, centred, block centre at y 1480. Each phrase pops in: scale 0.94 to 1 and opacity 0 to 1 over 4 frames.
- **Labels and titles:** SF Pro Rounded Bold 40 px, white on a smaller orange pill, top left at x 60, y 230 on photos.
- **Accent:** burnt orange `#D9531E`.
- **Terminal:** neutral `#121212`, text `#F0F0F0`, dim `#8A8A8A`, SF Mono at the size that fits 44 columns with 44 px padding, line height 1.35, panel corners 28 px, inset 36 px. Highlight: the same orange pill grows behind the target text (scale-x from its left edge over 5 frames), text stays white.
- **End card:** his words in SF Pro Rounded Heavy 72 px white; the link below on an orange pill in SF Pro Rounded Bold 48 px. Over his held frame dimmed 45%. Centred.
- **Motion:** lively. Pops over 4 frames, scroll 10 frames ease-out back.
- **Colour feel:** warm, saturated, golden highlights, like the reel's kitchen.

## C. One family (a designer's proposal he did not ask for)

Everything on screen speaks the terminal's language, so the subtitles, labels, card and terminal read as one system made by one hand.

- **Subtitles:** JetBrains Mono ExtraBold, 62 px, white, left-aligned at x 60 like terminal text, each line on its own tight band `rgba(11,13,16,0.82)` with 10 px padding (like selected text). At most 26 characters a line, one or two lines, never a single word alone on the last line, block centre at y 1470. Phrases cut in.
- **Labels and titles:** JetBrains Mono Bold, 34 px, upper case, white with a 14 px blue square before it, top left at x 60, y 230 on photos.
- **Accent:** electric blue `#3D8BFF`.
- **Terminal:** full-bleed panel edge to edge with no corners, `#0B0D10`, text `#E7E9EC`, dim `#6C7480`, JetBrains Mono at the size that fits 44 columns with 60 px padding, line height 1.35. Highlight: a blue selection block sweeps across the target left to right over 5 frames, text white on blue.
- **End card:** his words in JetBrains Mono Bold 60 px white, the link in blue below at 44 px, left-aligned at x 60, over his held frame dimmed 55%.
- **Motion:** crisp. Hard cuts, block sweeps, no fades; scroll 8 frames, ease-out.
- **Colour feel:** neutral to slightly cool, a little more contrast, true whites.
